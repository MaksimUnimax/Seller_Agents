## Current structural pattern after three families

The SERPs now show two simultaneous patterns:

1. **shared category core** — dual-marketplace products/pages rank for generic, Ozon and WB queries;
2. **marketplace-specific depth** — the same or other domains also rank dedicated Ozon/WB pages with marketplace-native tasks/API vocabulary.

Examples of paired depth:

- JAFO: shared home + separate Ozon and WB blog pages;
- SuperIntellect: separate Ozon and WB guide/integration pages;
- Intly: separate Ozon and WB integration pages.

This is search evidence for a real generic+specific content pattern in the market. It is not yet an Octoport IA decision.

## Product-truth caution

A large part of the recurring competitor set promises mutation-heavy actions: price changes, ad management, stock/card updates, auto-replies, autonomous execution. Octoport launch scope is read-only seller data/report work through a chosen LLM, so these competitor promises are useful for vocabulary/category research but cannot be copied as launch claims.
