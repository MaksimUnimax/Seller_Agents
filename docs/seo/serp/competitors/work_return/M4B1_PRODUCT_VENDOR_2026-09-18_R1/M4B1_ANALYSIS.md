## Boundary findings

The corpus repeatedly demonstrates why competitive vocabulary cannot be copied into Octoport truth:

- Some competitors claim direct writes, automatic repricing, campaign control, bidder behavior or automatic review responses. These remain competitor claims and sit outside Octoport’s current read/analyze/recommend launch boundary unless separately authorized.
- Product-card coverage mixes operational analysis with creative text/image generation. Creative generation is not silently treated as Octoport core.
- Integration pages can show that a third party connects OpenAI/ChatGPT with a marketplace; they do not prove Octoport capability or source access.
- Competitor references to “official API,” partner status, security or real-time behavior remain page claims, not verified Octoport facts.
- Ozon, Wildberries and Yandex Market observations are kept distinct. No marketplace capability is transferred to another marketplace.
