It does not expose:

- profit / net profit;
- margin / profitability;
- unit economics;
- income / expenses;
- payouts / cash flow;
- commissions / logistics / storage;
- product profitability;
- Ozon/Wildberries-specific finance language.

The dominant returned wording (`финансовый аналитик маркетплейсов` = 64) is easily interpreted as a human profession. The direct product/task wording is only 8 and has no child expansion.

This does **not** mean the underlying finance/profit job is weak. It means users likely phrase the task through concrete business outcomes/metrics rather than the abstract category `финансовая аналитика`.
